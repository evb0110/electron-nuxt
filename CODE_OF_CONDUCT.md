# Code Of Conduct

EVB Viewer aims to be a practical, welcoming project for people working on document viewing, OCR, PDF workflows, and desktop packaging.

Please:

- Be respectful and patient in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Focus criticism on behavior, code, evidence, and trade-offs.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections when something lands poorly.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discriminatory language, and unwanted personal attention.
- Keep private information private.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that make collaboration unsafe or unproductive.
